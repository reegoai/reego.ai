Product Web · Android · iOS

Shiftera builds a balanced shift schedule from your own rules. Team admins and planners enter the team, the people, their roles and time off, and rules such as rest periods, weekly hour caps and seniority pairing. Shiftera generates ranked schedules, shows which rules were broken, and releases the plan to staff. Staff sign in to see their own shifts, enter time off and swap shifts.

It is used by hospitals and emergency departments, security teams, support teams and any team that has to cover every hour and split the load evenly. It runs on the web at app.shiftera.io and in the Android and iOS apps, all on the same account.
